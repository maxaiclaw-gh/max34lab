Format rules

Pixels determine the container

  • PNG protects required transparency.
  • Opaque stills can use JPEG.
  • GIF and video receive a solid matte where equivalent alpha is unavailable.
Business controls

Delivery stays under user control

  • Final review precedes delivery.
  • Long-running work reports progress and can be cancelled.
  • Metadata is retained or scrubbed according to privacy settings.

WYSIWYG acceptance criteria

  1. Given a synchronized document, when preview and export render at different resolutions, then normalized photo and caption positions retain the same visual placement.
  2. Given 3D Pop, parallax or a frame shadow, when preview and export calculate clearance, then both use the shared frame-padding decision.
  3. Given reusable AI subject assets, when export runs, then it resolves document-owned artifacts or rebuilds them at export quality rather than depending on a transient UI cache.
  4. Given privacy controls that remove metadata, when the result is delivered, then restricted creation, location or camera data does not travel in the exported file.

Common questions

Why do exported photos sometimes look different from the preview in other apps?

Usually because the preview and the export are drawn by different code paths at different resolutions. Max Photo Frames renders both from one shared geometry contract, so positions, crop and layer order stay identical.

Does exporting share my location or camera data?

Only if you choose to keep it. Metadata is retained or scrubbed according to your privacy settings before the file is delivered.

What resolution do I get?

Exports render at delivery quality from the best available source. When AI subject effects are active the output has a practical ceiling, and the quality picker states this rather than quietly downscaling.
