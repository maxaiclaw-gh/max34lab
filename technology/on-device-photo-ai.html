Intelligence that stays on your device.

Advanced processing remains part of a familiar Studio workflow.

Business purpose

Create reusable scene structure for subject-aware composition.

The capability allows the product to blur or remove a background, lift a subject through the frame, place words behind it, and reuse the same creative structure during export and recovery.

Business rules

Rules that protect the experience

  • Subject separation runs on device.
  • A clear subject is a precondition, not a guaranteed outcome.
  • Reusable visible AI pixels require persisted asset references.
  • The live canvas must not own full-resolution source pixels.
Quality attributes

Non-functional expectations

  • UI work remains responsive during analysis.
  • AI assets survive document restoration.
  • Preview and export preserve subject identity and ordering.
  • Unsupported Simulator inference is treated as an environment constraint.

Acceptance criteria

  1. Given an eligible photo with a usable foreground subject, when subject-aware processing completes, then the document retains recoverable mask or cutout references for that subject.
  2. Given a document with persisted AI assets, when the project is restored, then subject-dependent composition can be reconstructed without relying on a process-global full-resolution image.
  3. Given a photo with no usable subject, when a subject-dependent outcome is requested, then the application provides a safe fallback rather than presenting a false successful result.
  4. Given the same synchronized document and profile, when preview and export render the subject stack, then behind content, subject cutout and front content retain the same semantic order.
  5. Given a look-only change such as a filter or frame colour, when the document updates, then structural AI discovery is not repeated unless the change invalidates the relevant artifact.

Common questions

How do I cut out the subject of a photo on iPhone?

Open the photo in Studio and turn on a subject effect such as 3D Pop or a background mode. The app finds the foreground subject on the device and creates a reusable cutout you can pop out of the frame or place text behind.

Does the AI work without an internet connection?

Yes. Scene understanding and subject separation run locally using Apple Vision, so they work offline and nothing about your photo leaves the device.

What if the photo has no clear subject?

The app tells you rather than inventing a result. Subject-dependent effects explain themselves or switch off safely, and the rest of your composition continues to work.
