Privacy-conscious by design.

Max Photo Frames is built as a lightweight, ad-free creative tool with no account required for the core workflow. Photos stay in the app’s normal device workflow rather than being collected for advertising or social profiling.

Schools and families should still follow their own consent, device-management and student-photo policies. Do not publish a child’s image without the permissions required by your school or local rules.
