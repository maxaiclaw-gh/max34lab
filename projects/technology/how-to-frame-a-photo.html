Notation

Two processes, modelled as pools and lanes.

Creating an export and keeping the work portable are separate processes with separate participants. Solid arrows are sequence flow inside one participant; dashed arrows are messages between participants. Diamonds are exclusive decisions, and the circle attached to a task is what happens when that task cannot complete.

A · Create and export a framed photo

Scroll the diagram sideways on a narrow screen.

What the decisions protect

Two ways in, one result.

The guided path and the direct path converge on the same recorded change, so choosing the simpler route never produces a lesser creation.

Why one render lane

Preview and export are the same work.

The canvas and the saved file are produced by the same composition step at different resolutions. That is what makes the saved result match the reviewed one.

The attached circle

A missing subject is a designed path.

When a photo has no clear subject to lift, the process routes to an explanation instead of inventing a cutout, and the composition continues without the effect.

The loop

Nothing saves itself.

Review returns to editing as many times as the person wants. Leaving the loop is always an explicit choice to save or share.

B · Keep the work portable

Order matters
The preset is saved on the device before the sync decision, so syncing is a consequence of saving and never a condition for it.
Both endings are success
Sync is optional and off unless it is turned on. The path that ends locally is a completed process, not a failure.
No account in between
Sharing a look is a file sent directly to a person. The other device checks it before importing, and neither participant needs an account with us.
Personal details stay put
Names, dates and other personalisation are not part of either flow. They belong to the device they were entered on and move only when the owner explicitly exports them.

Common questions

How do I add a frame to a photo on iPhone?

Choose the photo, pick a frame and canvas shape in the Style tab, then save. You can either follow the guided steps, which can each be skipped, or work directly in the Studio, Style, Caption, Decor and Hub tabs.

Can I change my mind after editing?

Yes. Editing is non-destructive: your original photo is never altered, and every change is recorded as a separate decision you can revisit, undo or export differently later.

What formats can I export?

A still image, an animated GIF, a cinematic video or a Live Photo, all rendered from the same composition you reviewed on the canvas.
