Privacy

Your photos stay yours.

Max Photo Frames is listed on the App Store as Data Not Collected. Here is what that means in practice.

📱

Everything happens on your device

Editing, subject separation and export all run locally. Your photos are never uploaded to us.

🙅

No account, no tracking

There is nothing to sign up for, and no third-party analytics watching what you make.

☁️

iCloud Sync is yours to switch on

If you turn it on, your presets and captions are copied to your own private iCloud so they appear on your other devices. Never your photos, never our servers, and one tap deletes the copy.
