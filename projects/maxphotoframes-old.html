Max Photo Frames

A creative photo-framing application combining AI editing, Apple Vision capabilities, filters and a distinctive 3D Pop composition mode.

Platform
iOS
Role
Product owner, designer, tester and AI-assisted builder
Focus
Creative imaging and reliable editing workflows
Status
Active development

The product idea

Max Photo Frames gives users a playful way to transform photos using frames, filters, AI-powered editing and spatial accessories. The aim is to make advanced image manipulation feel simple and visual.

Core capabilities

  • Load and frame a photo.
  • Remove backgrounds, blur areas and remove objects.
  • Restore the original image at any time.
  • Add Apple Vision-style accessories such as glasses or bunny ears.
  • Apply filters on top of the current composition.
  • Use 3D Pop to isolate a subject and extend it beyond the frame.

Design challenge

The application supports both the original-photo workflow and the 3D Pop workflow. Both paths need to preserve edits, support undo and restoration, survive app backgrounding and reopening, and remain stable under memory pressure.

Validation focus

Testing covers feature combinations, caching, project restoration, repeated image loading, app minimisation, reopening, export, low-memory behaviour and the consistency of applied effects across both editing paths.
