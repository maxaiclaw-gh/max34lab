Privacy Policy

Updated for Max Photo Frames Version 3.3 · Last updated: 24 September 2026

Introduction

This privacy policy applies to the Max Photo Frames app (“the App”). Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what information is handled by the App.

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, store, share, or have access to any of your personal information. The App is designed to work on your device. The one exception is iCloud Sync, which you can turn on yourself: it copies some of your own content to your own private iCloud account, never to us. That is described in the iCloud Sync section below. The App also asks Apple for the name of the place a photo was taken, which is described under Photo Details and Camera Settings.

Photo Access

The App requires access to your photo library to function. This access is used only for you to select photos for editing. Your photos are processed on your device and are never uploaded to our servers or viewed by us.

Photo Details and Camera Settings

When you open a photo, the App reads the details stored inside the photo file: when it was taken, the camera settings (such as shutter speed, aperture, ISO, focal length and camera model) and, if the photo has one, the place it was taken. These details are read and kept on your device. They are never put into a preset you share, a backup, a My Details file or iCloud Sync.

A caption that shows camera settings is saved as a placeholder, not as the numbers, so a preset shows the settings of whichever photo it is used on. The numbers are drawn on a saved photo only when you have placed them there yourself.

If a photo carries the place it was taken, the App asks Apple’s place-name service to turn those coordinates into a town name, so it can suggest it for a caption. Only the coordinates go to Apple, never the photo, and nothing is sent to us.

Your Logo

You can choose an image from your photo library to use as a watermark. The App keeps a copy on your device so it can draw it. A logo leaves your device only in these ways, each of which you start yourself:

  • Sharing a preset. A preset file carries any logo the preset uses, so the person you send it to sees your logo. Sharing that preset shares the image itself.
  • Backups and My Details files. A full backup carries the logos your presets use and the one set as your default watermark. A My Details file carries your default watermark’s logo.
  • iCloud Sync, if you turn it on. A logo that a synced preset uses is copied once to your own private iCloud account, as described below.

We never receive your logo.

In-App Purchases

The App offers optional in-app purchases for tips. These transactions are processed securely by Apple through the App Store’s official payment system. We do not receive or store any of your personal payment information, such as credit card numbers.

iCloud Sync

Max Photo Frames offers optional iCloud Sync so your saved presets and your caption library stay the same on every device signed in to your Apple Account. It is off by default and only turns on if you switch it on in the app.

What syncs when you turn it on

  • Your saved presets, including the styles they reference, any group name you have filed them under, and any logo image a preset uses as its watermark.
  • Your saved captions (“My Captions”), including their styling and where they are placed.

What never syncs

  • Your photos, your documents in progress and your exported images. These stay on the device that made them.
  • Your personal details used for personalised captions, such as names and important dates. These belong to the device you entered them on and move only if you deliberately export them to a file.

Where it goes, and who can see it

Synced content is stored in your own private iCloud account using Apple’s CloudKit, in the private database tied to your Apple Account. It is not sent to a Max34Lab server, and we cannot read it, browse it or recover it for you. Apple’s handling of that data is covered by Apple’s own privacy policy and iCloud terms.

Turning it off and deleting the copy

You can turn iCloud Sync off at any time in the app, under Hub. There is also a Delete iCloud Data action that removes the iCloud copy of your presets, captions and logos and switches sync off. Everything already saved on your device stays exactly as it is.

Because this content lives in your own iCloud account, it also counts towards your iCloud storage and follows whatever iCloud settings you have on your device.

Usage Data

The App does not use any third-party analytics tools to track your usage. All data, such as your saved preferences (for example, default frame colour and font), is stored locally on your device and is not accessible to us. If you turn on iCloud Sync, some of that content is also stored in your own private iCloud account, which is still not accessible to us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us via the support page.
