Ideas for classrooms and school communities.

Teachers can use framed photos to help students document a project, present a process or reflect on a shared experience. The app can also support family activities where an adult helps a child create the final image.

Project portfolios

Collect progress photos and add dates or short captions that explain how the work changed.

Visual storytelling

Turn an experiment, excursion or class activity into a small photo story.

Art and design prompts

Explore colour, composition, borders and typography as part of a creative lesson.

Celebration and reflection

Create a finished memory for a class display, family update or end-of-project reflection.

Print it for the wall

Pick a print size such as A4 or 8x10 under Frame → Print & Shape. The app tells you what the photo will give on that paper and saves it at 300 dots per inch, ready for the classroom printer.

Share one class look

Set the school logo as the watermark, save the look as a preset and send the preset file to colleagues. The logo travels inside it, so every class starts from the same design.

Privacy-conscious by design.

Max Photo Frames is built as a lightweight, ad-free creative tool with no account required for the core workflow. Photos stay in the app’s normal device workflow rather than being collected for advertising or social profiling.

Schools and families should still follow their own consent, device-management and student-photo policies. Do not publish a child’s image without the permissions required by your school or local rules.

Keep the creative focus on the work.
