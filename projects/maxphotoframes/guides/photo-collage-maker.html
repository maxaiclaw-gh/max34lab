Before you download

Max Photo Frames does not build multi-photo collages yet.

It frames one photo at a time, beautifully. Combining several photos into a single canvas is planned and is not in the app today. This page explains what you can do right now, so you can decide before you install anything.

What works today

One look, every photo.

Build the style once, save it as a preset, then apply it to each photo in a tap.

Get support

Make a set of photos look like a set.

This is the part Max Photo Frames is built for. A trip, a family event, a pet’s funny moments or a run of ordinary days: frame each photo with the same saved preset and the series reads as one piece of work, whether you post them as a carousel, print them or arrange them somewhere else.

Build the look once

Choose borders, canvas shape or print size, gradients, shadows and colour on a single photo until it is right. For a set you will print, pick one paper size so every photo prints alike.

Save it as a preset

Your look is stored and reusable. Apply it to the next photo in a tap, and every image in the set matches.

Add the context

Captions, dates, watermarks (text or your own logo) and countdown trackers say when, where or why each photo mattered.

Export each one

Save at the quality you choose in the Hub, or at 300 dots per inch when the preset has a print size. Arrange the finished images wherever you like.

What is planned

Multi-photo collage is on the roadmap: several photos on one canvas, each with its own frame and caption, with the whole composition exported as a single image. It is a genuine piece of work rather than a setting waiting to be switched on, so there is no date attached to it here. When it ships, this page will describe it rather than promise it.

Useful for sharing, made for keeping.

A consistent set works as a social carousel, a birthday memory, a travel recap or a private visual journal. Max Photo Frames keeps the creative workflow focused on your images, with no ads and no unnecessary photo tracking.
