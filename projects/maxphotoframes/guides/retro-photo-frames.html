See how it works

Make a photo feel like a keepsake.

Max Photo Frames is designed for people who want more than a quick filter. Choose a frame, tune the border and canvas, add a caption or date, then finish the image with colour, shadows and decorative props.

Retro-style layouts

Create a classic instant-photo feel with clean borders, soft colour and room for a caption.

Film border

A length of 35 mm film in colour negative, black and white or slide, with perforations that show the background through the holes and amber edge printing along the film.

Retro Wide

The landscape instant-film card: a wide window with the caption chin below it. Choose it in the Frame tab under Border, or start from the Retro Wide preset.

Camera settings on the film

The Film Landscape, Film Portrait and Film Card presets write the photo’s shutter speed, aperture, ISO and focal length on the film edge, as an ordinary caption you can restyle, move or remove.

Prints edge to edge

Pick a print size from 4x6 to A4 under Frame → Print & Shape. The frame fills the sheet with no white bands, the Retro card grows its chin to fit the paper, and the photo saves at 300 dots per inch.

Captions and dates

Add the words and details that make a photo meaningful, with placement you can control.

Layer Magic

Blur or separate the subject from the background to give a simple frame more depth.

Private by design

Max Photo Frames is built around on-device creativity and avoids unnecessary photo collection.

From casual snapshot to framed memory.

Use the app for holidays, family moments, pets, travel photos, daily photo journals or an image you want to share as a finished piece. The goal is not to make every image look the same; it is to help each photo arrive with a little more intention.
