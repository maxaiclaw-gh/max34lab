About the app

Max Photo Frames

Max Photo Frames is a free iOS app built to help you add elegant frames, captions, and styles to your photos. The app is lightweight, private, and easy to use: with no ads or tracking.

Languages and accessibility

The app is in English, Swedish, Korean and Chinese (Simplified and Traditional). It follows your iPhone’s language; to choose another, open the Hub and tap App Language.

Every screen works at the largest text size and with VoiceOver. If something is hard to read, cut off or not announced, please write to us with the screen and your text size or VoiceOver setting, and we will fix it.
