Privacy

Your photos stay yours.

Max Photo Frames is listed on the App Store as Data Not Collected. Here is what that means in practice.

📱

Everything happens on your device

Editing, subject separation and export all run locally. Your photos are never uploaded to us.

🙅

No account, no tracking

There is nothing to sign up for, and no third-party analytics watching what you make.

☁️

iCloud Sync is yours to switch on

If you turn it on, your presets and captions are copied to your own private iCloud so they appear on your other devices. Never your photos, never our servers, and one tap deletes the copy.

Multi-device

Your presets, ready everywhere.

Turn on iCloud Sync to keep My Presets and My Captions ready on your iPhone and iPad. Or use one backup file that you control.

☁️

iCloud Sync

Turn it on and your presets and captions appear on every device signed in to your Apple Account. Edit on iPhone, then pick up on iPad.

📦

One-file backup

Save all your presets, captions and settings to a single file. Move to a new phone without losing a thing. Choose what to restore.

🔒

Photos stay private

Only your design choices travel: frame style, colours, effects and caption placements. Never the photo itself.
